Experience the Thrill of Nature's Power

At Karura Forest Whitewater Rafting, our purpose is to connect adventure seekers with the untamed beauty of nature through exhilarating and safe river experiences. Our mission is to provide unforgettable moments that foster teamwork, courage, and environmental appreciation.

History

Founded in 2010, Karura Forest Whitewater Rafting Company began with a simple mission: to share the thrill of Kenya's majestic rivers with adventure seekers from around the world. What started as a small family operation has grown into one of East Africa's premier rafting destinations, serving thousands of thrilled adventurers annually while maintaining our cherished family values and personal touch.
